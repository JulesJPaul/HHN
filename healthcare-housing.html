One place to start when housing and care overlap.

Healthcare Housing Network is the umbrella connecting Jules Paul's housing, transition-support, and shared-living work. Through OSHS LLC — One Stop Health Shop, the network helps people identify practical resources as they move toward community living.

Service availability depends on individual needs, eligibility, location, and third-party providers. This website does not represent emergency, medical, legal, or financial advice.
