Healthcare Housing Network

One network built around the realities of community living.

Healthcare Housing Network is the umbrella connecting Jules Paul's housing, real estate, transition-support, and shared-living work. The goal is to help people not only find a place to live, but also identify the support systems that can make community living safer and more sustainable.

OSHS LLC — One Stop Health Shop

OSHS focuses on community transition and resource coordination. This includes assisting people leaving facility-based settings as they re-enter the community, as well as helping community members connect with housing, transportation, medical-service access, financial resources, and other supportive services.

Healthcare Housing Network and OSHS LLC provide housing, transition, and resource-coordination support. Service availability depends on individual needs, eligibility, location, and third-party providers. This website does not represent emergency, medical, legal, or financial advice.
